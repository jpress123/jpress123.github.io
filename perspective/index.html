Principles behind these suggestions

Composition guidelines are starting points. A centered arrangement, an asymmetric crop, or an expansive view supports different intentions. Empty-looking space must already exist in your subject. Review overlaps and edge crops yourself.

For a mirror still life, select a region within the mirror as your focus. Try the downward view, then check whether existing table edges and reflections lead toward it. The app does not identify mirrors or reconstruct their reflections.

Review geometry

Color regions preserve visible positions and proportions at the original viewpoint. Check each form against your photograph. Depth is an editable estimate. Background regions describe the setting.

From photograph to drawing

Automatic simplification groups nearby colors into polygons. These regions may split an object or join similar objects. Select a region and choose a box, cylinder or ellipsoid when appropriate. Its initial visible bounds stay aligned with your photograph.

One photograph cannot supply exact hidden geometry or real-world measurements. This tool uses a shallow, editable scene for perspective studies. Large viewpoint changes expose the limits of that approximation.

Freeze locks the current canvas crop, proportions and line settings. Print produces a page-sized guide labeled with your intended canvas dimensions. For transfer, use the same width-to-height ratio. Printing is fitted to paper rather than full-size tiled output.
