How to use this view in your sketch
  1. Trace two shelf edges running in the same direction. Extend them toward their shared point, even beyond the page. Repeat across the shelf. Both points sit on the horizon.
  2. Follow the upright edges toward the third point below the scene. Compare their tilt with the sides of your bottle.
  3. Sketch the mirror rim first. Mark where the bottle overlaps it and where its reflection meets the glass. Use the spoon as a path from the foreground.

This construction interprets the round base as a flat mirror and the upright oval as a frame. Dimensions and hidden surfaces are estimated from one drawing.
