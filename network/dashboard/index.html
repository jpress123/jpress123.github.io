Reporting rules报告规则

  1. Results are reported figures and carry the label "as reported by the practitioner" unless a third party verified them.
  2. Figures are added together only when they share a unit and a definition. Different units appear side by side.
  3. Each figure links to its story, and each story links to its evidence.
  4. Each figure carries its period and an as-of date.
  5. Empty cells show zero coverage, so gaps stay visible.
  1. 成果为报告数字，除非经第三方核实，均标注“实践者自报”。
  2. 仅在单位和定义一致时才汇总数字，不同单位并列显示。
  3. 每个数字链接到对应案例，每个案例链接到其证据。
  4. 每个数字均注明统计期间和截至日期。
  5. 空单元格显示为零覆盖，以便保留空白。
