Innovation Opportunities创新机会

A city, community, company, NGO or school states a need. The editor matches it with proven solutions from the signals and stories, writes a rationale for each match, and introduces an expert from the network.城市、社区、企业、非政府组织或学校提出需求，编辑从信号与案例中匹配经过验证的方案，为每个匹配撰写理由，并引荐网络中的专家。

  1. A need is posted through the form.通过表单发布需求。
  2. Needs and solutions are scored on shared tags: sector, solution type, DSF zone, scale and conditions.按领域、方案类型、DSF 区域、规模和条件等共同标签为需求与方案评分。
  3. The editor reviews the top three and writes a rationale for each.编辑审核前三个候选并为每个撰写理由。
  4. An expert accepts the introduction through the contact route.专家通过联系渠道接受引荐。
  5. The editor tracks the status and records the outcome.编辑跟踪状态并记录结果。
