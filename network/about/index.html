Who we are关于我们

SUES Design Network convenes the sustainable solutions that already exist, makes them visible in a common structure, and moves them to the people who can adopt them.SUES 设计网络汇聚已经存在的可持续方案，以统一结构呈现，并将其传递给能够采用它们的人。

Mission使命

The network follows the Designing Sustainable Futures position that preferred futures are built from existing solutions, not invented from nothing. It finds and verifies signals of sustainable practice, documents stories of impact, and connects people who have a need with people who hold a solution.

本网络秉持“设计可持续未来”的立场：理想的未来由已有的方案构建，而非凭空发明。我们发现并核实可持续实践信号，记录影响力案例，并将有需求的人与拥有方案的人连接起来。

The DSF approachDSF 方法

Every entry receives two coordinates. Production runs from local, meaning made, grown or generated near the user, to global, meaning made in networks that span regions. Consumption runs from individual, meaning chosen and used by one person or household, to collective, meaning shared, pooled or provided by a community, an institution or a public service. The editor answers two fixed questions from the source: where is most of the value produced, and who consumes it.

每条记录都有两个坐标。生产轴从本地（在用户附近制造、种植或产生）到全球（在跨区域网络中制造）；消费轴从个人（由个人或家庭选择和使用）到集体（由社区、机构或公共服务共享、汇集或提供）。编辑依据来源回答两个固定问题：大部分价值在哪里产生？由谁消费？

How the network works运作方式

  1. Each morning a scheduled agent scans sources in several languages and opens every source URL before using it.每天早上，定时代理以多种语言扫描来源，并在使用前打开每个来源链接。
  2. The agent writes two signals a day to the data file and drafts the weekly digest material.代理每天向数据文件写入两条信号，并起草每周简报素材。
  3. Members submit signals, stories, needs and events. Every submission waits in an editor queue.会员提交信号、案例、需求与活动，所有提交均进入编辑审核队列。
  4. The editor checks every source, strength, impact score and zone, then approves and publishes.编辑核对每个来源、强度、影响力评分与区域，然后批准并发布。

Editorial standards编辑标准

A signal is concrete, current, verifiable, and tied to a place and a practitioner. A trend piece, an opinion column or a target without an action behind it does not qualify.

Greenwashing guard.

  1. A company's own figure is labelled as the company's own.
  2. An emissions or savings claim needs its unit, baseline and period, or the entry says they are missing.
  3. A pledge or target is rated Early at most, until delivery is shown.
  4. An entry names what remains unverified.

Signal strength. Early: research stage, a single pilot or lab. Emerging: announced, multiple pilots, or policy alignment. Accelerating: in the market, paying users, deployed at scale. When unclear, the editor chooses the lower tier.

Impact score, 1 to 5. Strategic urgency, not source prominence: 1 niche, 2 sector-shaping, 3 industry-defining, 4 cross-industry, 5 systemic.

People and places. Practitioners are named only with consent, or when the source already names them publicly. Communities and local knowledge holders are credited. A solution from one place is never presented as ready for another without a transferability note.

Corrections. A correction note with its date stays visible on the entry. A retracted record stays in the archive with a retracted flag.

AI use. An AI agent drafts entries from verified sources. A human editor approves every entry before publication.

信号须具体、当前、可核实，并与特定地点和实践者相关。趋势评论、观点专栏或没有行动支撑的目标不符合条件。

防“漂绿”规则。

  1. 企业自报数字标注为企业自报。
  2. 减排或节约声明须注明单位、基线和期间，缺失时须在条目中说明。
  3. 承诺或目标在兑现前最高评为“早期”。
  4. 条目须说明尚未核实的内容。

信号强度。早期：研究阶段、单一试点或实验室。兴起：已公布、多个试点或政策配套。加速：已进入市场、有付费用户、规模化部署。不确定时选择较低层级。

影响力评分 1 至 5。衡量战略紧迫性而非来源知名度：1 小众，2 影响领域，3 定义行业，4 跨行业，5 系统性。

人物与地点。仅在获得同意或来源已公开署名时提及实践者。尊重并致谢社区与本地知识持有者。未附可迁移性说明时，不将某地的方案描述为可直接用于他处。

更正。更正说明及日期保留在条目上。撤回的记录保留在档案中并标注撤回。

AI 使用。AI 代理依据经核实的来源起草条目，每条内容发布前均由人工编辑审核批准。

Design Harvests 3.0: next actions设计丰收 3.0：下一步行动

Design Harvests has 18 years of experience in urban-rural design. After the Chedun Town Workshop in March 2026, expectations for its third phase range from an event and an expo, to speedy delivery of the Chedun Riverside Rural Innovation Leisure Belt, design training for project workers, research projects, immersive learning for students, and Business-to-Place services. Two next actions follow.

设计丰收在城乡设计领域已有 18 年经验。2026 年 3 月车墩镇工作坊之后，各方对第三阶段的期待包括：活动与博览会、尽快落实车墩滨河乡村创新休闲带、面向项目工作者的设计培训、研究项目、面向学生的沉浸式学习，以及“企业对地方”服务。由此确定两项下一步行动。

A

Design Harvests Hub设计丰收枢纽

A physical and virtual knowledge ecosystem with continuous outputs: concepts, cases and community. Participants include change agents, policy actors, developers, Business-to-Place consultants and investors, corporates, start-ups such as AgriTech, adjacent research fields, and special interest groups on biofinance, bioregioning, systems thinking and the design industry.实体与线上结合的知识生态系统，持续产出概念、案例与社群。参与者包括变革推动者、政策制定者、开发者、“企业对地方”顾问与投资人、企业、农业科技等初创企业、相关研究领域，以及生物金融、生物区域、系统思维与设计产业等专题小组。

B

Semi-nomadic festival半游牧式节庆

A three-day festival of pop-up events across the region around Chedun Town: on a farm, at the market, in a factory, by a river. Each evening everyone meets in one place to eat together and discuss the day. On the last day, each participant shares the relationships they plan to establish or strengthen. The format could be replicated in other regions along the Green Belt and Road. Dates to be confirmed.为期三天、分布在车墩镇周边的快闪活动：农场、集市、工厂、河边。每晚大家齐聚一处共餐，交流当天所见所感。最后一天，每位参与者分享计划建立或加强的合作关系。该形式可复制到“绿色一带一路”沿线其他地区。日期待定。
