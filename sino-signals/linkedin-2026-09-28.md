Is Washington now treating Beijing as an equal partner in AI safety, or just buying time on chips?

Xi Jinping's state visit to Washington closed Friday with the White House announcing a US-China Super Intelligence (SI) Dialogue, a bilateral channel for AI incidents modeled on Cold War-era hotlines. Vice-Premier He Lifeng and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent completed the first round in New York on September 20, confirmed by China's Commerce Ministry on September 24. The next session is set before November, alongside a $30 billion tariff cut.

Why it matters in China: Beijing negotiated from parity, not catch-up. Xi told Trump the two nations share "the capability and responsibility to develop and manage AI for good," and Wang Yi framed the summit as putting relations on "equal footing." The channel gives China a forum to contest US chip export restrictions and "distillation" accusations at head-of-state level, not through unilateral controls.

Global impact: this is the first formal government-to-government AI incident channel between the two AI superpowers, but its scope is undefined. Neither side has said what counts as an "SI incident." It signals Washington now treats Beijing as a dialogue partner on frontier AI safety, a framing that will shape the EU, UK, and other Safety Institutes' own China tracks.

Monday morning: if you run a dual US-China AI strategy, read this as a signal that export-control enforcement could ease incrementally, not as detente. Have your government affairs team watch the November session for the first real test of what this channel does.

Separately, China's Financial Product Online Marketing Management Measures take full effect September 30. Douyin has scrapped its "financial advisor" certification, removing 6,888 accounts and blocking 1.5 million-plus cross-border financial promotions this quarter. Xiaohongshu has removed 1.01 million violating notes and 157,000-plus accounts in six months. The sequence, licensed institution first, then credentials, then marketing, shifts financial content authority off influencers and onto regulated institutions.
