When an AI animation makes a geometry proof look obvious, how does a teacher find out which step a pupil missed?

At Bole Experimental School in Jinan, teachers used a November 5 workshop to build interactive materials showing how to derive the volume of a cylinder. They worked on a specific explanation that colleagues could inspect and revise together. The school described what teachers made. How pupils reasoned with the material remains an open question.

A shared lesson gives a school something concrete to ask of its technology budget. Two animations can arrive at the same formula while making very different demands on a learner. Pause each one before the final step and ask a pupil to predict what comes next.
