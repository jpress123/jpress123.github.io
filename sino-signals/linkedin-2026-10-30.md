What happens when AI filmmaking has to earn an audience, rather than impress a demo room?

Bona's AI-assisted feature Sanxingdui: Future Past opened in Chinese cinemas on October 23. National Business Daily reported weak early attendance. JETRO describes a production process where AI generated imagery while people retained creative planning and production decisions.

The important shift in China's entertainment industry is the move from generated clips into a full cinema release. That brings storytelling, character continuity and audience expectations into the same test.

For creative leaders elsewhere, the lesson is practical: faster image generation is only one part of making a film people want to watch. The release establishes a route to market; it does not establish profitability or lasting audience demand. Creative judgment still needs a clear owner, and success needs measures beyond production output.

Thinking futureback about this signal, consider running a small audience test of an AI-assisted sequence. Compare story comprehension, emotional engagement and revision effort. Give a named creative lead responsibility for the final cut.
