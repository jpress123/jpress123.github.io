# Pending approval

Sino-Signals | November 6, 2026

What if the next mass-market AI breakthrough is how people buy it?

On November 2, Qwen Office CEO Chen Yusen joined Li Jiaqi’s livestream to sell a monthly AI membership for RMB 0.01. Sina reports nearly 100,000 purchases in roughly half an hour. Demonstrations included generating an interactive fishing map.

The distinctive move is retail design: a familiar host, concrete tasks, a recognisable subscription and an established checkout turn abstract AI capacity into something shoppers can understand and try.

China’s livestream-commerce infrastructure gives AI providers a ready-made route to consumer attention. For Western retailers and service designers, the question is how to make useful capabilities legible without disguising limitations or future costs.

These are heavily subsidised promotional purchases, not evidence of lasting demand. Renewal and repeated use will matter more than the launch count.

Thinking futureback about this signal, what would it take for Western brands to teach customers what AI can do through everyday experiences? If they leave that job to platform owners, who will define the relationship, the expectations and the price?
