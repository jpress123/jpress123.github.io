What happens to your livestream studio, your on-camera talent, and your production budget when a competitor can spin up a lifelike host from a single photo?

The result lip-syncs, gestures, and can pick up objects, sing, and dance, running on iFlytek's Spark model with long-term-memory personas. Tencent and Baidu shipped rival real-time systems the same month, and Baidu claims a 74 percent cut in production cost.

A one-photo avatar removes the last setup barrier, so the tool spreads from big brands to small merchants across every major platform.

The open question is whether Western platforms match single-photo real-time generation or cede the low end to Chinese vendors.
