What good is an AI parking recommendation if you reach the gate without a valid permit?

For Shanghai’s import expo, a visitor can ask Xiaojiao about parking in English or Chinese, by voice or text. The assistant opens the reservation service. The driver chooses the lot and time, enters a plate number and confirms. Back in the conversation, the visitor can retrieve the booking or get walking directions from the car park to the venue entrance.

One detail matters. Visitors are instructed to print the permit in colour and display it in the vehicle. The journey moves from conversation to reservation to a piece of paper that someone at the gate must recognise.

Thinking futureback about this signal, would a Western city pay a supplier for answering the question or for getting the traveller through the gate? Who has the authority to change that last step? If nobody owns it, what happens to the visitor who arrives with a confirmed booking on a phone and no access to a printer?
