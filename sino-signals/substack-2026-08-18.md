# Sino-Signals | Tuesday, August 18, 2026 — The Cheap Tokens Are Gone and the Agents Are Talking to Each Other

*China's most-used model just ended the token price war, and Alipay published the protocol that lets one company's agent transact through another company's checkout.*

Two announcements landed within a day of each other in Hangzhou, and together they describe a market moving from land grab to infrastructure. One raises the price of intelligence. The other standardizes how intelligence gets paid.

## DeepSeek Ends China's Token Price War With Peak Hour Surge Pricing

DeepSeek's new API price list took effect at midnight Beijing time on August 17, 2026, replacing flat rates with peak and off peak billing. Peak hours run 9am to noon and 2pm to 6pm Beijing time, and off peak costs half of peak. Flagship DeepSeek-V4-Pro now bills 9 yuan per million cache miss input tokens at peak, up 200 percent, and 27 yuan per million output tokens, up 350 percent, while cache hit input rose 1100 percent to 0.3 yuan from 0.025 yuan. Off peak rates are 4.5 yuan, 13.5 yuan, and 0.15 yuan. The company posted the change on August 13, the same day it shipped the production release of V4-Pro with stronger agent capability.

DeepSeek is rationing scarce domestic compute with a lever borrowed from electricity grids, pushing batch and overnight work out of the daytime peak so the platform stops falling over. It crashed repeatedly in May 2026 as user volume grew 66.7 percent against 8.3 percent growth in compute reserves. Tencent Cloud, Alibaba Cloud, and Baidu Intelligent Cloud had already raised API prices, and Zhipu has raised them three times this year while call volume still grew 400 percent.

DeepSeek-V4-Flash was the single most called model on OpenRouter for the week of August 3 to 9, at 8.83 trillion tokens, and Chinese models have topped that platform for 15 consecutive weeks. Hualong Securities reads the move as the formal end of a year of loss making price competition among Chinese labs. Western teams that rebased production traffic onto Chinese APIs on cost grounds now watch the floor move under them.

## Alipay Launches China's First Cross Vendor Protocol for Agent Commerce

At its first AI ecosystem partner conference in Hangzhou on August 17, 2026, Alipay released a full stack commercial base for merchant agents plus the AHA protocol suite, short for Agent Hub Access, covering skills interfaces, agent to agent interconnection, and device perception and execution. Its super service agent Abao has converted more than ten thousand services to agent callable form across ride hailing, food ordering, tourism, and government services since opening to testing in June. More than twenty partners signed on to co-build, including Qwen, Huawei, OPPO, vivo, Xiaomi, Honor, BYD, Geely, Li Auto, NIO, Dongfeng, and Rokid. Alipay is giving developers 100 million free tokens each and opening 57 proprietary commerce primitives including membership, coupons, and Zhima Credit.

Alipay is standardizing the layer where an agent on one company's device transacts through another company's merchant, and it owns the payment, identity, risk, and fulfillment rails that make the handoff settle. Abao already reaches handset brands holding more than 70 percent of the Chinese market and 16 automaker brands, with design wins at more than 60 automakers. Ant CEO Han Xinyi expects agent commerce to break out within 6 to 12 months, and OPPO reports its Xiaobu assistant added roughly 200 Alipay services and over 2 million new active users.

Western agent commerce still runs mostly inside single ecosystems, where an assistant completes a purchase within its own walled checkout. A cross vendor protocol backed by handset makers, automakers, and model providers at once creates a default standard before Western equivalents ship, and it turns the car cockpit and smart glasses into transaction surfaces. Whoever sets the handshake rules also sets the authorization, data isolation, and liability terms.

Monday morning: ask which of your services an external agent could call today, and decide whether you join an emerging interoperability standard or hold transactions inside your own app.

## The Pattern

Read together, these two moves mark the point where Chinese AI stops competing on how little it costs and starts competing on what it controls. DeepSeek is pricing compute like a utility. Alipay is claiming the settlement layer for machine-initiated purchases. The first decides what your agents cost to run. The second decides who collects when they buy something.
