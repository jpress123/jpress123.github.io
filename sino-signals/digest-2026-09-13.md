## The pick of the week

### Alibaba Sends AI Digital Employees Into ByteDance and Tencent Workplaces

**🌐 Platforms & Social | Accelerating | Impact 4 of 5**

Alibaba Cloud released a new version of QoderWake on September 8, 2026, letting a user create a working AI colleague from a one-line description of the role. Those agents now run inside all three major Chinese workplace platforms, Alibaba's DingTalk, ByteDance's Feishu, and Tencent's WeCom, pulling context autonomously from shared documents, calendars, and group chats. QoderWake ships 10 pre-tuned roles including product manager, data analyst, UI designer, and front-end and back-end developer. Since its April release, Alibaba says it has deployed close to 100,000 digital employees that executed roughly 2 million tasks over three months.

**Why it scales in China.** DingTalk, Feishu, and WeCom together reach most of China's white-collar workforce, so an agent that crosses all three inherits a distribution base no Western equivalent can match. Alibaba is deliberately abandoning the walled-garden habit at the application layer because the revenue now sits in workflow execution rather than in seat licenses. Chinese enterprises also adopt agent tooling with less procurement friction than US firms, which compresses the pilot-to-production cycle to weeks.

**What it means outside China.** Microsoft, Google, and Salesforce each sell agents that work best inside their own suite, and each treats that lock-in as the moat. Alibaba has priced interoperability as the feature instead, which resets what a buyer will accept. Expect procurement teams in Europe and Southeast Asia to start asking whether an agent runs across Teams, Slack, and Google Workspace, and to read a single-suite answer as a limitation.

**Monday morning.** Audit which of your AI agent contracts assume a single collaboration suite, and price what a cross-platform requirement would cost at renewal. Ask HR and learning teams which of the 10 roles Alibaba pre-tuned overlap with jobs you are currently hiring for, because that list is a public forecast of which knowledge-work tasks get automated first. Decide now whether your answer is redeployment or headcount reduction.

Source: [South China Morning Post](https://www.scmp.com/tech/big-tech/article/3366919/alibaba-sends-ai-digital-employees-work-rival-apps-bytedance-tencent)
