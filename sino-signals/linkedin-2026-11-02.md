What happens when local storytellers bring AI into the production team?

A studio in Licheng, Shanxi, has taken an AI-assisted cultural short drama to Youku. Shanxi Daily reports that 我在古黎当上仙 premiered on October 30, combining generated imagery with a fantasy story rooted in local heritage.

The interesting development in China’s entertainment industry is the connection between local creative knowledge and an established streaming outlet. A small team’s distinctive material becomes part of a contemporary screen format.

For cultural organisations elsewhere, the useful question is who shapes the story, checks its accuracy and decides what deserves to survive each revision. Faster image generation alone does not answer those questions. The report gives no production budget, audience results or evidence of increased tourism.

Thinking futureback about this signal, consider making one short scene with local storytellers and an accountable editor. Test whether viewers understand and remember the place, document permissions, and compare revision effort before expanding into a series.
