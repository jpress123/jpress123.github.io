What happens to your AI budget when the cheapest tokens in the world stop being cheap?

At midnight Beijing time on August 17, DeepSeek replaced flat API pricing with peak and off peak billing. Peak hours run 9am to noon and 2pm to 6pm Beijing time. Off peak costs half of peak, and it is still more expensive than last week. Flagship V4-Pro now bills 9 yuan per million cache miss input tokens at peak, up 200 percent, and 27 yuan per million output tokens, up 350 percent. Cache hit input rose 1100 percent, from 0.025 yuan to 0.3 yuan.

Why this matters in China. DeepSeek is rationing scarce domestic compute with a lever borrowed from electricity grids, moving batch work out of the daytime peak so the platform stops crashing. It went down repeatedly in May as usage grew 66.7 percent against 8.3 percent growth in compute reserves. Tencent Cloud, Alibaba Cloud, and Baidu Intelligent Cloud had already raised prices. Zhipu has raised them three times this year and call volume still grew 400 percent.

Global impact. DeepSeek-V4-Flash was the most called model on OpenRouter for the week of August 3 to 9, at 8.83 trillion tokens, and Chinese models have led that platform for 15 straight weeks. Hualong Securities reads this as the formal end of a year of loss making price competition among Chinese labs. Every Western team that moved production traffic onto Chinese APIs on cost grounds is watching its floor move.

Monday morning. Pull your token spend by hour, by model, and by cache hit rate. Move every batch, evaluation, and offline pipeline out of Beijing daytime hours. Then re-run the build versus buy math you closed when Chinese tokens cost a fifth of the alternative, because the arbitrage that justified the switch just narrowed. Treat cache hit rate as a first class engineering metric. That is where the steepest increase landed.
