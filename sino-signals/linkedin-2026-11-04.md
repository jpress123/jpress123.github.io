What should school leaders bring back from AI training?

In Hunan, 100 principals from Changsha, Zhuzhou and Xiangtan took part in a programme combining expert sessions, visits to three schools and collaborative planning. Ten groups presented ten school digital-transformation action plans.

The provincial education department reported the programme on November 3, following training held during October 25–30.

The useful development in China's education sector is the connection between leadership learning and school practice. Principals were asked to work through what AI means for teaching, staff support and management, with examples from real campuses.

For education leaders elsewhere, the question is what changes after the workshop. Who helps teachers when a tool fails? Who reviews its output? Which classroom problem is worth addressing first? The report documents participation and plans, not improved student outcomes.

Thinking futureback about this signal, consider running a small planning session with school leaders and teachers around one classroom problem. Define a supervised trial, staff support and review criteria before expanding it.
