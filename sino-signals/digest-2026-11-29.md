## The pick of the week

### China’s Retail Survey Exposes the Gap Between AI Deployment and Scale

**🏭 Intelligent Production | Emerging | Impact 3 of 5**

Deloitte China’s November 25 summary of its joint CCFA retail study reports that more than half of surveyed enterprises have deployed agents or use public generative AI applications, while more than eight in ten have not reached scale. The research draws on association members and partners, not a representative census of Chinese retail.

**Why it scales in China.** An industry association provides a channel for retailers to compare practices across firms. The report separates technical feasibility, real pilots and scaled operation, with permissions matched to risk.

**What it means outside China.** Western retailers should test whether a successful demonstration can survive different stores, staff and data conditions. Survey responses and consultant recommendations do not prove revenue gains or independently audited productivity improvements.

**Monday morning.** Thinking futureback about this signal, who would own the work of making an AI process repeatable across stores? Which incentives and decision rights must change, and whose agency is lost if deployment expands before staff can contest its decisions?

Source: [Deloitte China and CCFA](https://www.deloitte.com/cn/zh/Industries/consumer/perspectives/ccfa-gai-white-paper-2026.html)
