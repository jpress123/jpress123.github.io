## The pick of the week

### Golden Rooster Film Festival Asks AI Filmmakers to Show Their Working Process

**🎨 Creative & Culture | Emerging | Impact 4 of 5**

At its first AI film unit in Xiamen, the Golden Rooster and Hundred Flowers Film Festival announced its recommendations on November 9. Fujian Daily reports that 20 teams selected from 287 applicants completed a 72-hour filmmaking challenge. The festival’s published format called for teams to document the creative process throughout.

**Why it scales in China.** The festival paired a public showcase with a common assignment and production records. That gives other filmmakers material for studying how teams worked through the same constraints.

**What it means outside China.** Festivals and commissioners can ask which decisions were made in the edit, where source material came from and how contributors were credited. A finished sequence alone rarely answers those questions.

**Monday morning.** Ask a team to show two versions of a scene, identify who chose between them and explain what changed the decision.

Source: [Fujian Daily, via Fujian Provincial Government](https://www.fj.gov.cn/xwdt/mszx/202611/t20261110_7246829.htm)
