## The pick of the week

### Bona Takes AI Filmmaking Into Cinemas and Tests Audience Demand

**🎨 Creative & Culture | Accelerating | Impact 3 of 5**

Bona's AI-assisted feature Sanxingdui: Future Past opened in Chinese cinemas on October 23. National Business Daily reported weak early attendance on October 26; JETRO's October 29 account describes AI-generated imagery with creative planning and production decisions retained by humans. A commercial release is established, but audience acceptance and profitability are not.

**Why it scales in China.** The project connects generative production with an established cinema distribution channel. My interpretation is that creative teams now face a more demanding test than producing impressive clips: sustaining characters, narrative and audience attention across a feature.

**What it means outside China.** For film and entertainment leaders outside China, the useful comparison is the entire production and audience-development process. Generating footage does not establish that viewers value the story, and early box-office observations should not be treated as a final result.

**Monday morning.** Test a short sequence with viewers who are not told how it was made. Track story comprehension, emotional engagement and revision effort, and give a named creative lead authority over the final cut.

Source: [National Business Daily](https://m.nbd.com.cn/articles/2026-10-26/4618530.html)
