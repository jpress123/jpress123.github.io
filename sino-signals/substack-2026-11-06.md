## Nanjing Bank Uses AI to Prepare Industry Financing Conversations

At a November 3 finance roadshow, Bank of Nanjing demonstrated AI support for understanding industry customers and matching financing proposals. Its account describes a pre-visit one-page briefing, agents that capture experience, and integration of industry data.

The municipal event brought financial institutions and technology companies together around application needs. The bank's example locates AI within relationship preparation and sector analysis rather than a standalone customer chatbot.

For banking leaders, the organisational question is how staff check and challenge machine-prepared views of a business. The bank's own report does not quantify credit quality, time savings or customer outcomes.

Examine a pre-meeting briefing workflow with relationship managers. Require traceable evidence and human review of financing suggestions before expanding its role.

Bank-authored account of a November 3 demonstration, published on a November 4 URL. Not evidence of autonomous credit approval or measured lending performance.

## Qwen Turns AI Subscriptions Into Livestream Shopping

On November 2, Qwen Office CEO Chen Yusen joined Li Jiaqi's livestream to sell a one-month AI membership for RMB 0.01. Sina reports nearly 100,000 memberships sold in about half an hour, alongside demonstrations including an interactive fishing map.

The offer connects AI services to an established shopping host and checkout experience. Our interpretation is that familiar retail conventions can make unfamiliar digital services easier to try.

Consumer brands can study how demonstrations and packaging translate technical capabilities into understandable tasks. Promotional purchases do not establish sustained use, renewal or profitable demand.

Review how customers first experience an AI service. Test whether a practical demonstration and clear membership terms improve understanding, then measure use after the promotion ends.

Event November 2; Sina report November 3; archived November 6. Sales figures are reported livestream figures, not independently audited retention.
