Your cloud provider promises green AI compute by 2030. Have you checked the number behind the promise?

21st Century Business Herald reported this week that China's computing centers drew 170 billion kilowatt-hours in 2025, just 1.6% of national electricity use, citing National Energy Administration data that projects roughly 800 billion kilowatt-hours by 2030 as AI expands. Tencent, Alibaba, China Mobile and China Telecom are racing to lock in renewable supply to cover the gap. Tencent already sources 48.5% green power, Alibaba tops 34%, and Kuaishou's self-built data centers run at 93%.

Why it scales in China. An April 2026 four-ministry action plan already commits Beijing to fully clean-powered AI infrastructure by 2030. That lets the state pair new compute capacity with new renewable generation on one national timetable and route western wind and solar to eastern data centers through mandated power purchase agreements, a scale of coordinated green procurement fragmented Western grids can't easily match.

Why it matters for Western leaders. Microsoft, Google and Amazon face the same AI power crunch, minus the centralized machinery to match build-out to renewables on a fixed schedule. If Chinese cloud providers reach near-total green AI compute by 2030, it becomes a real lever in cloud contracts and ESG-sensitive procurement worldwide.

Monday morning. Benchmark your own renewable procurement against the published green-power percentages above, and treat power availability, not chip allocation, as the binding constraint in 2027-2030 capacity planning.

Also today: Unitree Robotics has fallen 44% since its August 19 Hong Kong listing, erasing roughly $30 billion in market value. China's securities regulator has begun steering humanoid robot makers away from public listings until they show recurring revenue, leaving at least six firms, including Deep Robotics and Leju Robot, waiting on IPO plans. Only 9% of Unitree's 2025 revenue came from industrial sales, a benchmark now shadowing every humanoid funding round in China and the West alike.
